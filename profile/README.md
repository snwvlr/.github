## ◆ &nbsp; KORPPI MAIL

<i>If it isn't in RAM, it doesn't exist.</i>

<br/>

<p>
Disposable email built on the opposite principle of every disposable email service: nothing on disk, no analytics, no plaintext tokens. Mailboxes, messages and attachments live <b>only in volatile memory</b>, behind <b>512-bit cryptographic tokens</b>. A reboot wipes the entire service. By design.
</p>
